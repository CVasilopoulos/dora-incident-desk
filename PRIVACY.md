# Privacy policy: DORA Incident Desk

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

DORA Incident Desk is a Forge app that runs entirely on Atlassian's platform ("Runs on Atlassian").

- **What it stores:** incident facts entered by users (classification inputs, times, report status, report draft links), app settings, and the Register of Information pasted by an administrator. It does not store personal data such as names, email addresses or Atlassian account IDs.
- **Where:** in Forge key-value storage on the customer's own Atlassian site. Report drafts are written as Confluence pages or Jira comments on the same site, with the permissions of the user who drafts them. The register is published to the site's Teamwork Graph when an administrator connects the connector.
- **Egress:** none. The app sends no data outside Atlassian and uses no third-party services or analytics.
- **Retention and deletion:** data stays until the app is uninstalled; uninstalling removes the app's Forge storage. Confluence pages and Jira comments created by users remain under the site's own retention.
- **Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/CVasilopoulos/dora-incident-desk/issues.
